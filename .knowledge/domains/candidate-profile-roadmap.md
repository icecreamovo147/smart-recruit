# Candidate profile roadmap (P3 deferred)

The following enhancements are intentionally out of scope for the P0–P2 candidate profile upgrade:

- Structured skills with category, proficiency, and years of experience
- Certificates and language ability modules
- Candidate-owned structured project history (P2 may preview parsed projects but does not persist them)
- Deeper alignment between candidate profile fields and job matching requirement profiles

Candidate-maintained truth sources after P0–P2:

- `candidate_profiles` plus `candidate_educations` / `candidate_experiences`
- HR AI resume intelligence remains in `resume_*` tables and is not directly editable by candidates
